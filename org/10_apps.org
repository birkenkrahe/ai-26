1. *GMail*
   - Good prompt:
     #+begin_quote
     Using my Gmail:

     Goal: Identify emails that require action

     Input: My last 10 unread emails

     Output:
     - Sender
     - One-line summary
     - Required action (if any)

     Constraints:
     - Ignore promotions and newsletters
     - Be concise
     - Do not invent missing details
     #+end_quote
   - Combo prompt (GMail + Google Calendar):
     #+begin_quote
     Using my Gmail and Google Calendar:

     Goal: Create a weekly action plan

     Step 1:
     - Identify important emails requiring action

     Step 2:
     - Check my calendar for available time this week

     Step 3:
     - Suggest when to handle each email task

     Output:
     - List of tasks
     - Suggested time slots

     Constraints:
     - Do not overlap with existing events
     - Be concise and structured
     #+end_quote
